# 🚀 Yuanly AI v1.0.0 - Official Release

## Welcome to the Future of Travel in Turkey!

Yuanly AI is now officially live on the Apple App Store. We are bridging the gap between the wonder of Turkey and the digital habits of the Chinese market.

### ✨ Key Features in v1.0.0:
- **Rednote-Inspired Discovery:** A visually stunning feed to explore Turkey's "Golden Experiences".
- **AI Voice Concierge:** Our "Yuanly Orb" handles your bookings and questions in real-time, in your language.
- **Seamless Payments:** Full integration with WeChat Pay and Alipay for a friction-free experience.
- **Secure Access:** Enterprise-grade security with 2FA and JWT.
- **Merchant Ecosystem:** A powerful dashboard for Turkish operators to manage their business.
