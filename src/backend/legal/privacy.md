# Privacy Policy - Yuanly AI (缘旅)
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-04

## 1. Introduction
Yuanly AI helps travelers discover and reserve tourism experiences in Turkey. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## 3. Data We Collect
- **Account information:** name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), preferred language, optional phone number.
- **Reservations:** the experience, date/time, number of guests, the contact name/phone/notes you enter, and the reservation status.
- **AI Concierge:** the text messages you send to the in-app assistant, used to answer your request and improve recommendations.
- **Notifications:** in-app messages about your reservations.
- We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking.

## 4. How We Use Data
- To create your account, show and manage your reservations, and let the company you reserve with confirm them.
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
- To provide AI-assisted search and recommendations.

## 5. Sharing
Your name, contact details, notes and reservation details are shared only with the company (tour operator) you reserve with, so it can serve you. Our hosting providers process data on our behalf. We never sell personal data.

## 6. Payments
The current version does not process online payments. You pay the company directly at the venue.

## 7. Retention and Your Rights
You can edit your profile in the app and delete your account at any time from **Profile → Delete account**. Deletion removes your personal data (name, email, phone, notifications, AI history); reservation records are kept in anonymised form for the companies' accounting. You may also request a copy of your data or corrections by email.

## 8. Children
The service is not directed to children under 16.

## 9. Changes
We will update this page when our practices change.
