# Yuanly AI - Web Platform Sitemap

The Web platform is divided into two distinct experiences: The Public Portal (for tourists) and the Corporate Dashboard (for merchants).

## 1. Public Portal (B2C)
Designed for pre-trip planning and SEO.
- **Home Page:** Hero section, Top Destinations, "How it Works", Featured Experiences.
- **Experience Catalog:** Filterable list of all activities (similar to GetYourGuide).
- **About Us:** Brand story, "The Yuan Concept", Trust & Security.
- **Support/FAQ:** Help center and AI-guided FAQ.
- **Login/Sign-up:** Synchronized with Mobile App via WeChat/Apple ID.

## 2. Corporate Dashboard (B2B)
The command center for Turkish operators.
- **Analytics Home:** Revenue, booking volume, customer satisfaction (CSAT).
- **Inventory Manager:** Experience editor, Price manager, Calendar availability.
- **AI Lead Center:** Overview of active AI bookings and human-intervention requests.
- **Payouts:** Billing history, upcoming payments, tax documents.
- **Company Profile:** Verification status, licenses, and digital portfolio.
