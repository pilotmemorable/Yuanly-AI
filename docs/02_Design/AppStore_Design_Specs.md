# Yuanly AI - Apple App Store Design Specifications

## 1. Design Philosophy: "Apple Elegance x Rednote Spirit"
To succeed on the App Store, Yuanly must not only be functional but must feel like a "native" premium iOS experience. We adhere to the **Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG)** while preserving the visual energy of Rednote.

### Core Design Pillars
- **Fluidity:** Every transition must feel organic. No hard cuts. Use of "Spring" animations.
- **Depth:** Implementation of Z-axis layering (blurred backgrounds, floating cards, and subtle shadows).
- **Precision:** Perfect alignment, generous white space, and pixel-perfect iconography.

## 2. High-Fidelity Screen Specifications

### A. The "Immersive" Onboarding
- **Visual:** A full-screen, auto-playing cinematic loop (4K) of a hot air balloon rising over Cappadocia.
- **Overlay:** A translucent glassmorphism layer at the bottom with the "Yuanly" logo in white.
- **Interaction:** A single, elegant "Get Started" button that uses a "Scale-up" animation on press.
- **Apple Integration:** Integration with "Sign in with Apple" for one-tap, secure onboarding.

### B. The " la la la " Explore Feed (Rednote Style)
- **Layout:** A refined Masonry Grid. Cards have a corner radius of 28px.
- **Haptic Touch:** Implementation of "Peek and Pop". Long-pressing a card shows a blurred preview of the experience and a "Quick Book" button without leaving the feed.
- **Visuals:** High-contrast imagery. The "AI-Recommended" badge is a subtle, glowing turquoise pill that pulses slowly.

### C. The "Yuanly Orb" Concierge
- **Visual:** The AI Orb is centered. It uses a **Metal-based shader** for fluid, iridescent movements that react to the user's voice frequency.
- **UI:** Messages appear as floating, soft-edged bubbles that fade in from the bottom.
- **Audio:** Integration with iOS's haptic engine; the phone vibrates subtly in sync with the AI's voice peaks.

### D. The "Seamless" Booking & Wallet
- **Payment:** A bottom-sheet (Half-Sheet) slides up for payment.
- **Apple Pay:** Primary payment option alongside WeChat Pay and Alipay for a native iOS feel.
- **Digital Ticket:** Once booked, a "Add to Apple Wallet" button appears. The ticket is designed as a luxury boarding pass with a QR code.

## 3. iOS Specific Technicals
- **Typography:** 
  - Primary: **SF Pro Display** (System font) for maximum readability and native feel.
  - Secondary (Chinese): **PingFang SC** for a modern, clean look.
- **Navigation:** A blurred, translucent Tab Bar at the bottom with custom-drawn, thin-line icons.
- **Dark Mode:** A deep "Midnight Blue" theme with Golden accents, ensuring a premium look for night-time browsing.

## 4. App Store Marketing Assets (The "Hook")
- **Screenshots:** 5 high-impact screens showing the "Discovery $\rightarrow$ AI Chat $\rightarrow$ Booking $\rightarrow$ Apple Wallet" flow.
- **App Preview Video:** A 15-second high-energy edit showing the fluidity of the app and the beauty of Turkey.
