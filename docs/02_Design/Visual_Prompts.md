# Yuanly AI - Visual Production Prompts

These prompts are designed for high-end AI image generators (like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Higgsfield) to create a consistent brand image.

## 1. Logo Design Prompts
- **Concept:** A fusion of the Chinese character "缘" (Yuan) and a futuristic digital knot.
- **Prompt:** 
  > "A high-end luxury logo for a tech company named 'Yuanly'. The logo is a minimalist, fluid, 3D, iridescent knot inspired by the Chinese character '缘'. Colors: Celestial Turquoise, Pearlescent White, and hints of silver. Set against a pure white background. Vector style, symmetrical, high-end jewelry aesthetic, 8k resolution, corporate luxury, ultra-modern."
