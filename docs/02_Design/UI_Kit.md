# Yuanly AI - UI Component Kit (The Visual Skin)

## 1. Component Library

### A. The "Experience Card" (The Rednote Unit)
- **Dimensions:** 320px x 480px (approx).
- **Background:** High-res image/video with a soft bottom-to-top black gradient (opacity 0% $\rightarrow$ 40%).
- **Corner Radius:** 28px.
- **Overlay Elements:**
    - **Price Tag:** Floating pill shape, Background: #C41E3A (Imperial Red), Text: White, Font: Semi-bold.
    - **AI-Badge:** Small glassmorphism pill, Background: rgba(64, 224, 208, 0.3), Text: #40E0D0 (Celestial Turquoise).
- **Animation:** Scale-up on tap (1.0 $\rightarrow$ 1.05) with a spring effect.

### B. The "Yuanly Orb" (AI Visualizer)
- **Shape:** A perfect sphere with a fluid, evolving surface.
- **Colors:** Gradient shift between #40E0D0 (Turquoise) and #FFFFFF (White).
- **Behavior:** 
    - *Idle:* Slow pulsing breath (opacity 60% $\rightarrow$ 100%).
    - *Listening:* Expanding waves of light emitting from the center.
    - *Thinking:* Rotating orbital rings around the sphere.
    - *Speaking:* Syncing the pulse frequency with the voice pitch.

### C. Buttons & Inputs
- **Primary CTA:** Full-width, rounded (30px), Gradient: #40E0D0 $\rightarrow$ #00CED1. Shadow: Soft glow of the same color.
- **Input Fields:** Borderless, bottom-line only (1px), Light Grey $\rightarrow$ Turquoise on focus.

## 2. Global Animations (The Pulse)
- **Page Transition:** "Fluid Morphing". Instead of sliding, the content seems to grow from the point of interaction.
- **Loading State:** A minimalist hot air balloon rising vertically with a "bounce" at the top.
