# Yuanly AI - Mobile UI/UX Specifications

## 1. General Design Principles
- **Visual Style:** "Modern Oriental Minimalism". High white space, soft shadows, rounded corners (24px+), and high-resolution imagery.
- **Interaction Model:** Gesture-heavy. Swipe-to-explore, long-press for quick-view, pull-down to refresh.
- **Accessibility:** Large, readable typography for the 36-46 age group, while maintaining a "trendy" look for the 16-25 group.

## 2. Key Screen Specifications

### Screen A: The "Discovery" Feed (Rednote-Style)
- **Layout:** Masonry grid (staggered columns) of activity cards.
- **Card Elements:**
    - Full-bleed image/video.
    - Bottom-left: Activity title (e.g., "Fethiye Paragliding") + Star rating.
    - Bottom-right: Price tag in CNY (¥).
    - Overlay: A small "AI-Recommended" badge for personalized matches.
- **Interaction:** Tapping a card expands it into a full-screen immersive experience.

### Screen B: The "AI Concierge" Interface
- **Layout:** Minimalist chat interface. A clean white background with a soft gradient at the bottom.
- **Centerpiece:** A pulsating "Yuanly Orb" (the visual representation of the AI). When the AI speaks, the orb reacts with fluid animations.
- **Input:** Large, intuitive microphone button at the bottom center.
- **Quick-Chips:** Floating buttons above the mic for common requests (e.g., "Book a Tour", "Translate", "Find Hotel").

### Screen C: The "Booking" Flow (Frictionless)
- **Step 1: Date/Time Selection:** A horizontal scrolling calendar with "AI-Suggested" slots highlighted.
- **Step 2: Option Selection:** Package tiers (Basic, Premium, VIP) presented as elegant cards.
- **Step 3: AI Confirmation:** A brief summary generated by the AI ("You're booking the VIP Flight for tomorrow at 8 AM. Shall I proceed?").
- **Step 4: Payment:** Integrated WeChat Pay/Alipay sheet popping up from the bottom.

## 3. Navigation Transitions
- **Page Transitions:** Slide-in from right for deeper levels, slide-up for modals.
- **Loading State:** A subtle, branded animation of a hot air balloon rising slowly.
