# Yuanly AI - Interaction Prototypes (The "Life" in Action)

This document demonstrates how the AI agent actually "lives" and interacts with the user in real-world scenarios.

## Scenario 1: The "Dreamy" Discovery
**User (19yo, Gen Z):** *(Taps on a paragliding video)* "This looks amazing! Is it safe?"

**Yuanly AI:** 
*(Orb pulses softly in turquoise)*
"It is absolutely safe and breathtaking! We only work with certified pilots who have over 1,000 flight hours. Imagine gliding over the turquoise waters of Ölüdeniz while the wind carries you... it's a feeling of total freedom. Would you like me to check the best slots for you tomorrow?"

## Scenario 2: The "Efficient" Booking
**User (34yo, Millennial):** "I want a balloon tour for 2 people this Saturday morning."

**Yuanly AI:** 
*(Orb pulses rapidly, querying API)*
"Checking the Cappadocia skies for Saturday... ☁️ I have one premium slot left at 05:15 AM. It's the perfect time for the first light. Shall I hold this spot for you for 15 minutes while we finalize the details?"

**User:** "Yes, please."

**Yuanly AI:** 
*"Done! I've locked the slot. Total for two people is ¥1,200. I'm sending the WeChat Pay request to your screen now. Just one tap, and your dream morning is secured."*

## Scenario 3: The "Crisis" Management (The Human Touch)
**User (42yo, Gen X):** "The weather looks bad, can I cancel my flight?"

**Yuanly AI:** 
*(Orb shifts to a calming, slow white pulse)*
"I understand your concern. Safety is our absolute priority. Our pilots are currently monitoring the wind. If the flight is cancelled by the authority, you will get a full refund automatically. However, if you'd like to move it to Sunday, I can handle that right now. Would you prefer a refund or a reschedule?"
