# Yuanly AI - Merchant Onboarding & Management

## 1. The Merchant Journey (B2B)
To maintain high quality, merchants (Turkish tour operators) must go through a verification process.

### A. Registration Flow
1. **Application:** Merchant fills a simple form (Company Name, Tax ID, Category, Contact).
2. **Proof of Quality:** Uploads licenses, certifications, and 3-5 high-res photos of their experience.
3. **AI Vetting:** An internal AI agent analyzes the uploaded content and reviews (if any) to ensure it meets the "Yuanly Standard".
4. **Manual Approval:** A human admin performs the final check.

### B. Onboarding Setup
Once approved, the merchant sets up their "Digital Storefront":
- **Experience Creation:** Title, Description (translated into CN/EN by AI), Pricing, and Image Gallery.
- **Calendar Sync:** Integrating their existing booking system or using the Yuanly native calendar.
- **Payout Setup:** Providing bank account details for settlement.

## 2. Merchant Dashboard Features (Mobile & Web)
- **Real-time Alerts:** Push notifications for new bookings.
- **AI Lead Management:** The AI agent handles the first 80% of the conversation; the merchant only steps in for complex requests or live calls.
- **Performance Analytics:** Heatmaps of user interest, conversion rates, and revenue reports.
- **Dynamic Pricing:** Ability to offer "Early Bird" or "Last Minute" discounts.

## 3. Quality Control (The Trust Loop)
- **Rating Penalties:** Merchants whose ratings drop below 4.0 are automatically put on "Probation" and their visibility in the "Explore" feed is reduced.
- **Feedback Loop:** AI analyzes customer complaints and suggests improvements to the merchant (e.g., *"Customers often mention that the pickup point is hard to find; consider adding a video guide"*).
