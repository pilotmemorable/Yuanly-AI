# Yuanly AI - Initial Content Strategy (Launch Phase)

## 1. The "Golden" Experiences
To launch with impact, Yuanly will not list every tour, but a curated set of "Golden Experiences" that perfectly match the 16-46 age group's desires.

### A. The "Aesthetic" Bucket (Gen-Z / Influencer Focus)
- **Cappadocia Dream Flight:** High-res drone footage, breakfast in a cave, sunrise views.
- **Fethiye Blue Glide:** Paragliding over Ölüdeniz with a focus on the "Turquoise" contrast.
- **Istanbul Secret Rooftops:** Guided tours of hidden cafes and galleries with a view of the Bosphorus.

### B. The "Comfort & Prestige" Bucket (Millennials / Gen-X Focus)
- **Pamukkale VIP Thermal Experience:** Private guides, luxury transport, and exclusive access to thermal pools.
- **Ephesus Private Journey:** A deep dive into history with an expert historian and premium catering.
- **Turkish Riviera Yacht Day:** Private yacht charter with gourmet dining and secluded swimming spots.

## 2. Content Curation Workflow
Every experience listed on Yuanly must pass the "Yuanly Aesthetic Test":
1. **Visual Audit:** Minimum 5 high-res images and 1 cinematic video (max 15s).
2. **AI Translation:** Descriptions are rewritten by the AI to evoke emotion, not just list facts.
3. **Vibe Tagging:** Experiences are tagged with "Vibes" (e.g., #Romantic, #Adrenaline, #QuietLuxury, #InstaWorthy).

## 3. User-Generated Content (UGC) Loop
To scale, Yuanly encourages users to become "Experience Curators":
- **The Reward:** Users who upload high-quality reviews (video + text) earn "Yuan Points".
- **The Perk:** Points can be used for membership upgrades or "Secret Experience" unlocks.
