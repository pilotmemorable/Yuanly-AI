# Yuanly AI - Mobile User Stories

These stories define the core interactions for the mobile app, focusing on the "Mobile-First" philosophy.

## 1. The Tourist Journey (B2C)

### A. Discovery & Inspiration (The "Rednote" Experience)
- **As a Gen-Z Tourist (18yo),** I want to scroll through a visually stunning feed of "Turkey Experiences" (like paragliding in Fethiye) so that I can find activities that look "aesthetic" and trend-worthy.
- **As a Traveler,** I want to tap on an experience card to see a short video and real reviews from other Chinese tourists, so I can trust the quality of the activity.
- **As a User,** I want to save experiences to my "Wishlist" via a simple heart gesture, mirroring the Rednote interaction.

### B. AI-Driven Booking (The "WeChat" Utility)
- **As a Busy Traveler,** I want to click a "Book Now" button and, if the merchant is unavailable, have an **AI Voice Agent** guide me through the process, ask for my date/time, and confirm my booking.
- **As a User,** I want to pay for my paragliding tour using **WeChat Pay** or **Alipay** with a single biometric confirmation (FaceID/TouchID).
- **As a Member,** I want to receive a digital QR-ticket in my app that I can show to the provider upon arrival.

### C. Social Sharing & Interaction
- **As an Influencer,** I want to share my booking confirmation or a "coming soon" teaser to my **Rednote** or **WeChat Moments** with a single tap to excite my followers.
- **As a Customer,** I want to upload a photo/video review of my activity directly from my mobile gallery to help other travelers.

## 2. The Merchant Journey (B2B - Mobile Managed)
- **As a Small Business Owner,** I want to manage my availability calendar from my phone so I can accept or reschedule bookings on the go.
- **As a Provider,** I want to receive a notification when the AI Agent has successfully completed a booking for me, including the customer's details.
- **As a Merchant,** I want to update my "Digital Portfolio" (photos/videos) via the mobile app to keep my profile fresh.

## 3. Membership & Security
- **As a Guest,** I want to explore the app and see prices without creating an account, but be prompted to join for "Member-Only" discounts.
- **As a User,** I want to log in using my WeChat ID for a one-tap seamless onboarding.
- **As a Corporate User,** I want a 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication) prompt when changing payment settings to ensure my funds are secure.
